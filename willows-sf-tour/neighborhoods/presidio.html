The Presidio & Crissy Field

Military history, bay views, and one of SF's best walks

01 Oak's introduction

The Presidio is the best argument for the National Park Service. What was a military base for 219 years is now a forest, a marsh, a beach, a trail network, and a collection of repurposed military architecture — all tucked into the northwestern corner of the city with the Golden Gate as a backdrop. The Crissy Field walk is the finest urban waterfront walk I know: you start at the marina, walk through a restored tidal marsh, and end at the base of the Golden Gate Bridge. Do it in the late afternoon when the light comes off the water.

02 History & character

A military installation for 219 years under three flags — Spanish, Mexican, and American — before being transferred to the National Park Service in 1994. Crissy Field was a military airstrip restored to a tidal marsh in 2001. The transition from army base to park is one of urban America's great civic success stories.

03 Proposed itinerary

9:30 am
Start at Crissy Field Center
Get oriented. The marsh restoration is visible from the path — look for the egrets and plovers.
10:00 am
Walk Crissy Field to Fort Point
The full waterfront walk. Fort Point is directly under the bridge. Stand inside and look up at the underside of the span.
11:30 am
Golden Gate Bridge south tower
Walk out onto the bridge if the weather is good. The views east and west are completely different and both essential.
1:00 pm
Lunch at Presidio Social Club
In a converted military barracks. Good food, the setting is unlike anywhere else in SF.
2:30 pm
Walt Disney Family Museum
One of the best biography museums I've encountered. Even if you're ambivalent about Disney, the story is fascinating.
4:00 pm
Baker Beach
The north end has a dramatic bridge view. Walk south for more privacy.

04 Recommended spots

Fort PointLandmark
Civil War-era fort directly under the bridge. The view upward at the span is disorienting and beautiful.
Walt Disney Family MuseumMuseum
Serious biography museum, not a Disney store. Genuinely moving.
Presidio Social ClubFood
In a converted military barracks building. Excellent cocktails, good food.
Crissy FieldOutdoors
Restored tidal marsh and waterfront trail. The best urban walk in SF.
Baker BeachBeach
The north end has the best bridge view from a beach in the city.
Moraga Steps and tiled stairwaysOutdoors
The ceramic tile mosaic stairway. Worth the climb.
