Outer Sunset

Fog, surf culture, and the city's most underrated restaurant strip

01 Oak's introduction

Unless they're going to the beach, Most San Franciscans think of the Outer Sunset the way they think about Danville. But it has some of the same charms as any beachside town, along with some standout restaurants, bars and shops. This would be fun to do after an overnight at my house, especially in the early Fall when we get our warm season. Also: shorebirds.

02 History & character

Developed in the early 20th century as the city pushed west through the dunes. Long considered a sleepy residential backwater, the Outer Sunset has become a destination in its own right, with a thriving independent restaurant and café scene along Irving and Judah, and a surf culture centered on Ocean Beach.

03 Proposed itinerary

9:30 am
Morning walk on Ocean Beach
Go early before the fog burns off — or embrace the fog. The beach at 7am with surfers in the water is a defining SF image.
10:30 am
Sutro Baths
Walk up from the beach. The ruins of the 1894 public bathhouse are dramatic at any time. The cave at the back.
11:30 am
Coffee at Andytown
Neighborhood roaster in the Outer Sunset. The Irish Coffee here is excellent even in the morning.
12:30 pm
Lunch on Irving Street
Outerlands for brunch/lunch (expect a wait, worth it). Or Thanh Long for Vietnamese.
2:30 pm
Walk Irving from 9th to 25th
The full stretch of the neighborhood's restaurant strip. Look at the surf shops, the bonsai nursery, the strange mix of things.
4:00 pm
Sunset at Ocean Beach
If the weather cooperates. One of the best sunsets in the Bay Area — the sun drops directly into the Pacific.

04 Recommended spots

Outerlands Food
The Sunset's culinary anchor. Seasonal, wood-fired, excellent. Weekend waits are long.
White Cap Bar
Very nice craft cocktail bar at the ocean-end of Taraval.
Andytown Coffee Coffee
Neighborhood roaster. The Snowy Plover (sparkling water, Turbinado syrup, espresso) is their signature.
Ocean Beach Beach
Cold, windy, dramatic, and beautiful. Bring a jacket. The surf is serious.
Sutro Baths Landmark
Ruins of a 1894 public bathhouse. Free, open always, remarkable.
Java Beach Café Coffee
At the Great Highway. Surfers, locals, good coffee, casual.
Gus's Community Market Market
The neighborhood grocery. Good prepared foods section for a picnic.
